# Contributing

This repository prioritizes evidence discipline.

Use evidence labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observed | Directly visible in an artifact, dataset, sample, log, or cited primary source. |
| Reported | Stated by a cited external source. |
| Assessed | Analyst judgment based on evidence. |
| Claimed | Stated by an actor, victim, vendor, or third party without independent confirmation. |

Do not overstate attribution. Keep citations close to claims. Prefer detection-ready outputs.
